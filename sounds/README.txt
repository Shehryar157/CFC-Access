CFC Access event sounds

Put WAV files here with these names. If a file is missing, CFC Access
speaks a short phrase instead.

  low_health.wav          your health drops below a quarter
  enemy_low_health.wav    the enemy's health drops below a quarter
  super_ready.wav         your super meter becomes full
  enemy_super_ready.wav   the enemy's super meter becomes full
  time_low.wav            10 seconds left on the round timer
  round_won.wav           you win a round
  round_lost.wav          you lose a round

Only WAV files work, and one plays at a time.
